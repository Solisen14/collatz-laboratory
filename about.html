WHY THIS LABORATORY EXISTS

Explore the pattern.
Keep asking why.

Begin with any positive integer. Divide by two if it’s even, or multiply by three and add one if it’s odd. Repeat. The conjecture says that every positive starting number eventually reaches 1.

The rule is easy to follow. The proof remains elusive. Here, animations, interactive trees, experiments, and fractal explorations invite you to notice surprising behaviour and ask deeper questions.

An important distinction: Visual patterns and computational experiments can suggest ideas. They do not replace mathematical proof.
