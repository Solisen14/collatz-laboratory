THE COLLATZ LABORATORY · BEHIND THE PROJECT

A simple rule.
Two curious educators.

The Collatz conjecture begins with two small instructions, yet leads to questions nobody has been able to settle. We built this laboratory to make those questions visible, interactive, and worth exploring.

WHY THIS LABORATORY EXISTS

Why this laboratory exists

The Collatz Laboratory was created as the final project for MATH 674.004: Fractals, a course in the Master of Mathematics for Teachers program at the University of Waterloo.

Whether you are an educator, a student, a mathematician, or simply someone who loves learning something new, we hope you have taken your time here: exploring the Collatz conjecture, following unexpected patterns, and pondering its deep connections to other areas of mathematics.
