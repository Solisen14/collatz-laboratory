THE MATHEMATICAL QUESTION · CHAPTER 15

The Collatz Conjecture.

Begin with any positive integer.

A SINGLE CONNECTED TREE

IF EVENDivide by 2n ÷ 2
IF ODDTriple it, then add 13n + 1

Repeat these rules. The conjecture is that every positive integer will eventually reach 1.

From there, the sequence cycles through 1 → 4 → 2 → 1. No one has proved this happens for every positive starting value.
