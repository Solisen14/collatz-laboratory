Explore a circular tree built from real forward Collatz orbits. The value 1 is the central bullseye, with distinct number paths branching outward toward the rim. Select any node to follow its orbit back to 1.

How many starting integers?

Cyan when selected: even step, divide by 2 Gold when selected: odd step, 3n + 1 White labels: terminal values on the rim

How to read the circle. The black 1 is at the exact centre. Coloured dots are distinct integers, and fine grey connections link consecutive Collatz values. The terminal branches reach the outer ring, with white endpoint labels. Position and spacing make the branching structure visible; they do not encode the integer's magnitude. Follow the arrows of a selected orbit inward toward 1. A dashed return arc from 1 to 4 closes the familiar cycle.
