Circular Representation

See a circular tree built from real forward Collatz orbits. The value 1 sits on the right-hand edge; unique number paths branch inward and around the circle. Selecting a node traces its orbit to 1.

How many starting integers?

Cyan when selected: even step, divide by 2 Gold when selected: odd step, 3n + 1 White labels: terminal values on the rim

How to read the circle. Black 1 sits at the right edge. Each coloured dot represents a distinct integer; fine grey lines join values that are consecutive Collatz iterates. The white labels identify the terminal branches. The layout positions come from the branching network rather than numerical size or iteration count. The dashed return arrow illustrates 1 → 4, completing the repeating cycle.
