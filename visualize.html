How to read the circle. The numbered points around the rim are clickable starting markers. Inside, each visited integer occupies one shared node. Values with more steps remaining to reach 1 are placed farther outward. Radial distances are compressed to make long orbits visible, and the layout curves are illustrative rather than geometric measurements.
